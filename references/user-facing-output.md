# User-facing output

These rules are mandatory for both the Preliminary Financial Baseline and Monthly Review.

## Story before statement

Write about the user's life with money, not about schema fields. Lead with what the numbers mean for the user's choices, habits, safety, and goals. Use only the numbers needed to support that explanation. A user should understand the main conclusion within 30 seconds.

Do not reproduce the profile as prose. Avoid consecutive sections that merely enumerate income, rent, utilities, subscriptions, assets, and calculated metrics. Compress stable or ordinary facts; explain unusual, consequential, or uncertain ones.

## Voice and visual hierarchy

Use warm, spoken language in the user's language without becoming childish or overly familiar. Describe consequences rather than morally judging spending. Use a small number of meaningful emoji headings—such as `💰`, `🏡`, `🌱`, `🎯`, or `🧭`—to make the report scannable; do not decorate every line.

Titles should sound like a human description, not a meeting minute, accounting document, or database label. Generate them from the user's real circumstances instead of filling a fixed naming template.

## Interpretation and evidence

Keep facts, deterministic calculations, Agent interpretation, and recommendations distinguishable, but do not force each into a long standalone data section. Every interpretation must point to specific evidence. Keep estimates and unknowns visible without letting caveats dominate the report.

The Baseline `Financial Portrait` and Monthly `Monthly Life Pattern` are different features and cannot replace each other. For Baseline, use the defined types, dimensions, evidence, projection, and format in [financial-portrait.md](financial-portrait.md). For Monthly Review, use the free-form month-specific pattern in [monthly-review.md](monthly-review.md). Neither is a diagnosis or permanent identity.

Recommendations may contain zero to three actions. Do not manufacture work to appear useful; “暂时不用改变什么” is valid when supported by the facts.
