# Financial Portrait

This is a user-facing financial behavior portrait, not a psychological diagnosis or validated personality test. It describes current evidence and may change when income, spending, goals, or assets change.

## Two dimensions

Assess without fake precision:

- **储蓄能力**: low / medium / high / unknown, based on usable income and spending or observed accumulation.
- **目的清晰度**: low / medium / high / unknown, based on whether money has user-stated jobs such as daily life, safety, study abroad, career change, or long-term use.

If spending is unknown and there is no other reliable evidence of saving, use `探索型` as the primary portrait. Do not infer a spending type from income alone.

## Portrait names

Choose one primary portrait and, when useful, one secondary trait. Never force a label when the evidence is weak.

### 老实人攒钱型

Stable income and sustained surplus. Explain: “你不靠花活，主要靠每个月踏踏实实把钱留下来。” Do not imply that spending less makes the user morally better.

### 想要就要型

An identifiable life goal is motivating regular saving. Explain: “你不是为了存钱而存钱，而是有一个真正想实现的未来。” This means goal-driven accumulation, not impulsive shopping.

### 人生是旷野型

Experience, travel, hobbies, or social spending is a meaningful part of the observed pattern. Explain without calling it wasteful: “你愿意把钱换成体验、自由和当下的生活。”

### 过冬仓鼠型

Cash or deposits dominate and safety/liquidity is clearly prioritized. Explain: “手里有钱会让你安心；下一步可以考虑给部分钱安排更明确的任务。”

### 钱钱迷路型

There are assets, but their purposes are unclear or money is scattered across categories. Explain: “钱不是没有，只是还没有分工。” This is especially suitable as a secondary trait.

### 探索型

Income, spending, assets, or goals are too incomplete or unstable to support another portrait. Explain: “现在还不是给自己贴标签的时候，我们先把财务生活看清楚。”

## Output

```markdown
## 你的财务画像

### 主类型：钱钱迷路型
一句话：钱不是没有，只是还没有分工。

### 两个维度
- 储蓄能力：较强 / 中等 / 暂时未知
- 目的清晰度：较低 / 部分清晰 / 较高

### 为什么这样判断
- [specific fact]
- [specific fact]

### 如果保持现在的节奏
数字版：每月留下 ¥X，五年约新增 ¥Y；说明是否排除收益和大额支出。
人生版：把 ¥Y 翻译成用户关心的选择，例如旅行、学习、转行缓冲或留学准备金，不默认指定品牌或商品。

画像可信度：高 / 中 / 低
```

Calculate `Y` as `X × 60` before writing the report. Use ranges when X is a range. The five-year projection is a simple contribution illustration, not a promise, and excludes investment returns and unplanned spending unless explicitly modeled.

Use “月光倾向” only when sufficiently complete spending data shows spending near or above income. Do not use it as a character judgment.
